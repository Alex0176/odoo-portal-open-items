1. Open the contact (a person with portal access) and tick *Show open items in portal* (next to *Website*;
   visible to users with the right *Billing*).
2. The portal user now sees the tile *Open items* in *My Account* and can open *Open items*.
3. Choose the date in *Open items as of* and press *Show*. *Download PDF* creates the list for the chosen date.

Open items of the contacts of the same company are included, items of other companies never are.
The list is built from posted receivable entries. For a past date, a payment counts from the day of its
reconciliation, so the list shows what was unpaid on that day. Entries posted or cancelled later than the date
are taken as they are now.

A negative amount is a credit in favour of the customer (credit note, or a payment not yet allocated to an invoice).
Check the open items of a company in the back office before enabling the option.
