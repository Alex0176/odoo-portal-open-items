Lets selected **portal users** see the **open items** of their whole company: unpaid invoices, credit notes and
payments on account, with due dates, overdue days and the balance.

* The list covers all contacts of the commercial entity of the logged-in user (also archived colleagues).
* Any **cut-off date** (Stichtag) can be chosen; the list then shows what was open on that day.
* The list can be **printed** or downloaded as a **PDF**.
* It is switched **off by default** and enabled per contact (*Show open items in portal*), so nothing becomes
  visible to customers unasked.
